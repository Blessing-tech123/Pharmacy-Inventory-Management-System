# Pharmacy-Inventory-Management-System
A data-driven Pharmacy Inventory Management System built in Google sheets using advanced formulas, automation, and interactive dashboards for business decision-making.

## Introduction 
The Pharmacy Inventory Management System is an automated business management solution built with Google sheets. It helps Pharmacies and other business efficiently manage inventory, record sales, monitor expenses, track deliveries, and analyze business performance through an interactive dashboard. The system minimizes manual calculations by automating inventory updates, sales analysis and key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Problem statement
Many pharmacies and small business still record their inventory, sales, and daily expenses manually. This make it difficult to know the current stock level, track sales, monitor expenses, and calculate profit accurately. As the business grows manual record-keeping becomes time-consuming and increases the risk of errors.

This Pharmacy Inventory Management System was built to solve these problems by automating inventory tracking, sales recording, expenses management and business reporting. It provides an interactive dashboard with real-time insights, helping pharmacy and small business owners make better decisions, reduce manual work and manage their business more efficiently.

## Recommendations
To maximize the benefits of this Pharmacy Inventory Management System:

- Keep inventory records up to date by recording every stock addition and sale promptly
- Maintain accurate product information including Brand, Category and Cost price
- Review the dashboard regularly to monitor sales performance, profit, and inventory levels
- Act on low stock alerts early to avoid stock shortages
- Use the dashboard filters to analyze sales by month, brand, category, state and delivery status.
- Back up your Google Sheets file regularly to prevent data loss
- Train all staff members responsible for inventory or sales entry to ensure consistent and accurate data recording
- Periodically review business insights such as top-selling products, customer trends and delivery performance to support informed decision-making.
